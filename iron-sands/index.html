Field manual

How to play

  • 1Economy first. Build a Power Plant, then a Refinery. It comes with a Harvester that mines the gold ore and brings credits home.
  • 2Build in the dock. Tap a building to start it. When it says PLACE, tap it again, tap the map where you want it, then press Place here. New buildings must sit close to your base.
  • 3Army. Barracks train infantry, the War Factory builds vehicles. The Tech Center unlocks Heavy Tanks, Artillery, Level 3 upgrades and an Airstrike.
  • 4Orders. Tap a unit to select it, then tap the ground to move or tap an enemy to attack. Hold a finger down and drag to select a group, or press Army to grab everyone. Attack-move fights everything on the way.
  • 5Camera. Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, tap the minimap to jump.
  • 6Power. If you use more power than you make, everything builds at half speed and towers fire slower.
  • 7Win. Destroy every enemy building. Rocketeers and Cannon Towers beat tanks, Riflemen and Pillboxes beat infantry.
