Skyhoop

You fly for Tide with two teammates. Carry the ball, pass it around, and put it through the orange Ember ring. Guard your own teal ring.

Left thumb
Steer anywhere on the left half. Push up to climb, down to dive.
Shoot
Fire at the ring. Aim roughly at it and the throw finds the open side. Without the ball it becomes Tackle.
Pass
Pass to the teammate you face. When a teammate has the ball it becomes Call, and they pass to you.
Boost
Hold to fly faster. The ring around the button is your stamina.

Three-minute match. A tie goes to golden goal. Best with the phone sideways. Keyboard: arrows or WASD steer, Space shoots, E passes, Shift boosts, P pauses.
